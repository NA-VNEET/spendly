Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

Spendly ("the App") is a personal expense tracking tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the App:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes.

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank credentials, or any information from your financial institutions.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the App:

  • Displaying your expense history and spending summaries.
  • Authenticating your identity when you sign in.
  • Diagnosing bugs and improving app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core function.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secured database. We apply industry-standard safeguards including:

  • Password hashing — your password is never stored in plain text.
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Access controls limiting who can read your data.

No system is completely secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law.

6. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may use the following categories of third-party services solely to operate the App:

  • Hosting providers — to run the App's servers. These providers process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Font providers — Google Fonts is loaded from Google's CDN; font requests may be logged by Google per their own privacy policy.

We do not integrate with any analytics, advertising, or social media tracking services.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Portability — receive your expense data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in the Contact section below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information available on the App. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 7 business days.
